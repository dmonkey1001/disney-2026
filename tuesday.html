Before 5:00

  1. Sign in at disneyworld.disney.go.com on the laptop. Dining works in the browser.
  2. Open four tabs, one per restaurant below, each with its date, party of 9 and the Dinner meal period already set. Then each search is a single click at 5:00.
  3. Card ready — every booking is held against it for the no-show fee.
  4. Naming the nine guests in the seat dropdowns is optional. Generic “Guest 2” seats are fine, and naming people costs seconds you may need.

The one rule

Take a time inside the window at the first choice. If it only offers times outside the window, go straight to the second choice. Spend no more than about two minutes per restaurant before moving down the list — a good table at the backup beats a lost table at the favourite.

If the site won't take nine

  1. Book every restaurant for 8 instead, in the same order. That holds the tables.
  2. Citricos only: have Travis or Tai book 4 from their account at the same moment while you book 5. One account can't hold two overlapping reservations.
  3. At 6:00 Central, call (407) 939-3463, ask for Group Dining, and add the ninth to each booking.

Credit check
