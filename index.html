Why this order

Three scheduling facts did most of the work

  • Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party runs Dec 20 and Dec 22. On both nights Magic Kingdom shuts to day guests at 6:00 PM. Both are also sold out, so buying in isn't an option. That rules MK out on two of your four days and puts it on Dec 21 or Dec 23.
  • Hollywood Studios stays open to 9:00 PM only on Dec 20, 22 and 24 — Disney deliberately runs it late on party nights. On Dec 21 and 23 it closes at 7:00. So DHS has to be Dec 20 or Dec 22.
  • Saratoga Springs is a Deluxe Villa resort, which makes you eligible for Extended Evening Hours. EPCOT's December pattern is Mondays, 9–11 PM — including Dec 21. Two extra hours at a fraction of the crowd is the single best value in the week. Confirm it in the app once December dates post.

That leaves Animal Kingdom for Dec 20 by elimination — which happens to be the right answer anyway, since its 6:00 PM close gives you a gentle landing on your first full day after travel.

Things that will catch you out

Party of 9

Online booking caps at 8 guests. You must call (407) 939-3463 and ask for Group Dining. If they can't seat nine together, book two overlapping reservations and tell the host at check-in that you'd like to sit together — they usually manage it.

Gratuity

An 18% gratuity is added automatically for parties of six or more, and the dining plan does not cover it. Budget roughly $50–80 in tip per table-service meal — call it $300 across the week.

Transport

Saratoga Springs buses stop at five internal sections before leaving, so they fill up and take time. Budget 60–75 minutes door to gate in peak season, which is why the bus times above look so early.

Weather

Highs in the low 70s, lows in the low 50s. Evenings genuinely get cold once the sun drops — bring layers you can carry, and skip the water rides.

Park hopping

The old 2:00 PM restriction is gone. You can hop any time after tapping into your first park, subject only to capacity — which in Christmas week is a real possibility, so don't build a plan that depends on it.

Christmas Party

Dec 20 and Dec 22 are both sold out, so there's no option to buy in. It's not a loss — Dec 23 gives you the holiday parade, fireworks and decorations at no extra cost.

Verify in December

Park hours, Early Entry times and Extended Evening Hours all shift. The Dec 21 EPCOT evening session follows the published Monday pattern but hasn't been formally confirmed for December. Re-check My Disney Experience the week before you fly.
