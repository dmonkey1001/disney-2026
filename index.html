Why This Order

❄

Three scheduling facts did most of the work

  • Mickey's Very Merry Christmas Party runs Dec 20 and Dec 22. On both nights Magic Kingdom shuts to day guests at 6:00 PM. Both are also sold out, so buying in isn't an option. That rules MK out on two of our four days and puts it on Dec 21 or Dec 23.
  • Hollywood Studios stays open to 9:00 PM only on Dec 20, 22 and 24 — Disney deliberately runs it late on party nights. On Dec 21 and 23 it closes at 7:00. So DHS has to be Dec 20 or Dec 22.
  • Saratoga Springs is a Deluxe Villa resort, which makes us eligible for Extended Evening Hours. EPCOT's December pattern is Mondays, 9–11 PM — including Dec 21. Two extra hours at a fraction of the crowd is the single best value in the week. Confirm it in the app once December dates post.

That leaves Animal Kingdom for Dec 20 by elimination — which happens to be the right answer anyway, since its 6:00 PM close means the first full day after a travel day still ends at a civilised hour — and leaves the evening free to hop to EPCOT if anyone's still going.

The Lightning Lane Playbook

❄
Read this once and the day-by-day plan below will make sense.

There are two separate products, and the difference matters

  • Multi Pass is the one you buy per park day. You hold three selections at a time, and the moment you tap into the first one, you can add another. Do that all day and nine people realistically clear six to ten Lightning Lanes each.
  • Single Pass is bought per ride, and covers the five headliners Disney deliberately holds back: Seven Dwarfs Mine Train and TRON at Magic Kingdom, Guardians of the Galaxy at EPCOT, Rise of the Resistance at Hollywood Studios, and Avatar Flight of Passage at Animal Kingdom. No amount of Multi Pass gets you onto these.
  • Multi Pass is tiered at Magic Kingdom, EPCOT and Hollywood Studios: one Tier 1 plus two Tier 2 per booking. Animal Kingdom has no tiers — take any three. Tier assignments move around, so confirm in the app when you book.

What this means for the mornings: Lightning Lane does not replace rope drop, it redirects it. Pre-book the rides with the worst standby, then spend the first free hour on whatever you didn't get a pass for. Early Entry waits are low across the board, so that hour banks three or four extra rides while your first return window is still ahead of you.

Where the Credits Go

❄
Four dinners instead of five meals, and one of them is a proper one. Here's the arithmetic.

How the credits actually work

  • It's one shared pot, not per person per day. Nine guests over five nights gives us 45 table-service credits for the whole trip, spendable at any meal on any day.
  • Lunch and dinner cost the same. There is no separate lunch credit and no way to combine two of them into something better — a table-service lunch and a table-service dinner each cost one credit per person at the same restaurant. The only lever is how many table-service meals we book.
  • Signature restaurants cost two credits per person. For nine people that's 18 credits a meal instead of 9. So the real ceiling on credits alone is two signature meals plus one regular, and anything beyond that comes out of pocket.

Two things worth knowing at the table: buffets and prix-fixe menus charge a credit per person with no sharing, but at ordinary table-service restaurants two people may split an entrée. And the 18% large-party gratuity is never covered by credits, whichever level we pick.

Breakfast is the thin part — and the villas fix it

  • Both two-bedroom villas have full kitchens — stove, oven, full-size fridge, dishwasher, coffee maker, plus a washer and dryer. Lunch and dinner need no help. Disney counter portions are large, Animal Kingdom gets two of them, and Citricos is a serious meal. It is only the mornings that are underfed as the plan stands.

Things That Will Catch Us Out

❄
Parking

Standard theme park parking is free for Disney Resort guests — normally about $30 a day per vehicle, so roughly $240 saved across the van, the rental and four park days. Whoever is driving each vehicle needs their own MagicBand or Key to the World card at the booth, so don't let both of them end up in one person's pocket.

Magic Kingdom

The one park you cannot drive to. Parking is at the Transportation and Ticket Center, with security there and then a monorail or ferry across the lagoon — 30 to 45 minutes from car to gate. The resort bus goes direct. Take the bus on Dec 23.

The van and the rental

The van seats eight against a party of nine, so the rental is the ninth seat as well as a convenience. Two vehicles is the better arrangement regardless: people can leave a park early or stay late independently, which is exactly what this group is going to want. Park in the same row where you can, and photograph the row marker; the lots are enormous and every aisle looks identical at 10 PM.

Hopping and LL

A catch worth knowing before Dec 12: our first three Multi Pass selections must all be in the same park. Only once we tap into the first can we book one in a different park. So a morning hop costs us the pre-booked passes — hop after the first redemption, not before.

Pack a collar

Everyone needs a collared shirt and proper shoes in their suitcase. This is a before-you-leave-home item, not a day-of one — there is nowhere to buy your way out of it on Wednesday evening. Citricos enforces the signature dress code: no tank tops, flip-flops, cut-offs or torn clothing, and people do get turned away at the door. We go straight there from a full day at Magic Kingdom with no chance to change, so wear it from Wednesday morning. December makes that painless — a collar and closed shoes are comfortable at 70°F, and we want a layer for the evening regardless.

Party of 9

Online booking caps at 8 guests. We must call (407) 939-3463 and ask for Group Dining. If they can't seat nine together, book two overlapping reservations and tell the host at check-in that we'd like to sit together — they usually manage it.

LL for nine

Everyone must be linked in My Disney Experience before Dec 12, or you can't book them into a pass. One person books for the whole party. Nine people also need nine slots in the same return window, which is the first thing to run short on a busy day — so book the moment the clock turns 7:00, and take a slightly worse time over splitting the group.

Re-stacking

The single habit that decides how much we get done: the instant you tap into a Lightning Lane, open the app and book the next one. Hold three at all times. Done properly that's six to ten passes a day instead of three.

Gratuity

An 18% gratuity is added automatically for parties of six or more, and the dining plan does not cover it. Budget roughly $50–80 in tip per table-service meal — call it $300 across the week.

Weather

Highs in the low 70s, lows in the low 50s. Evenings genuinely get cold once the sun drops — bring layers you can carry, and skip the water rides.

Park hopping

The old 2:00 PM restriction is gone. We can hop any time after tapping into the first park, subject only to capacity — which in Christmas week is a real possibility, so don't build a plan that depends on it.

Christmas Party

Dec 20 and Dec 22 are both sold out, so there's no option to buy in. It's not a loss — Dec 23 gives us the holiday parade, fireworks and decorations at no extra cost.

Verify in December

Park hours, Early Entry times and Extended Evening Hours all shift. The Dec 21 EPCOT evening session follows the published Monday pattern but hasn't been formally confirmed for December. Re-check My Disney Experience the week before we fly.
